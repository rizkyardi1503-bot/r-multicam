v2.3.11.10 · PUBLIC · Windows & macOS · Signed offline license (required)

Offline use is now protected by a license record signed by the R Project server: the 72-hour offline grace trusts only that signature, so an edited license file no longer unlocks the plugin. Online use is unchanged; open the panel online once after updating. This update is required for every earlier version: use Check Update in the panel or the website download, and save and close Premiere before installing. It includes everything from v2.3.11.9 (music engine V19, visual quality checks, faster analysis).

v2.3.11.9 · PUBLIC · Windows & macOS · Music engine V19

A new beat tracker (music engine V19) follows one continuous beat chain through the whole song, so cuts stay on the beat on long DJ sets. The AI director now also checks each camera for brightness, sharpness and shake and avoids dark, blurry or shaky moments. Analysis is faster with identical results: AI Vision checks large 4K camera files about 2x faster, and a 4-camera 4K test went from 32 to 17 seconds. Prices in the panel match the website (Lifetime Rp2.000.000 one-time), the panel renews its sign-in before it expires, and a broken FFmpeg path falls back to a working one. On macOS the installer always uses the verified FFmpeg build; a Homebrew FFmpeg is only a fallback and is no longer copied. The update is optional (v2.3.11.2 and newer keep working): use Check Update in the panel or the website download, and save and close Premiere before installing.

v2.3.11.8 · PUBLIC · Windows & macOS · Buy Lifetime in the panel

Online lifetime checkout is open again. Buy Lifetime and Check Payment are back in the panel for signed-in accounts without a lifetime license, in the account bar and on the Sync Cameras to Music card. Buy shows the IDR amount and asks before it opens the Midtrans checkout in your browser; after paying, Check Payment activates the license (it also activates on its own when Midtrans confirms the payment). Everything else is unchanged from v2.3.11.7. The update is optional (v2.3.11.2 and newer keep working): use Check Update in the panel or the website download, and save and close Premiere before installing.

v2.3.11.7 · PUBLIC · Windows & macOS · Drone & FPV sync, exact Cut Pace

New in Sync Cameras to Music (formerly Sync to Master Music; beta, lifetime license only): drone and FPV clips without audio are placed from their camera clock, bridged through a camera that synced by audio, on their own [CLOCK] track. Correct one clip with Use selected clip and every clip of that drone follows; the correction is remembered per drone and shoot date, and Gyroflow FPV exports are remembered by their bin name. Cut Pace 2, 4, 6 and 8 now counts real beats, so cuts land exactly every chosen number of beats and stay on the beat when a second song starts. Apply is no longer blocked near drops, 2 Beat no longer holds one camera for a whole section, and when a camera's footage ends inside a shot the edit switches to the camera that continues on a shared beat.

The panel is now five numbered steps in working order: Analyze Music, Sync Cameras to Music, Auto Cut Multicam, Best Drops and Random Zoom. Music detection runs with built-in settings, and the optional online AI music review was removed, so analysis is always local. Also fixed: the tempo no longer locks at half speed, so Best Drop windows have the promised length; Continue Multicam works after a stop that emptied a camera track; Windows auto-update works with non-English letters in the user name; the macOS installer waits for Premiere to quit; and a pending update is shown after restarting Premiere. Online lifetime checkout is closed for now, so Buy Lifetime is hidden; trials and existing licenses are not affected. The update is optional (v2.3.11.2 and newer keep working): use Check Update in the panel or the website download, save and close Premiere before installing, and test on a duplicate project first.

v2.3.11.6 · PUBLIC · Windows & macOS · Sync to Master Music (beta) & faster cleanup

New: Sync to Master Music (beta, lifetime license) places every camera clip, including very short ones, at its position on the master music on A1, using each clip's own audio and the camera clock. It builds a new synced sequence and leaves your current sequence unchanged. Clips it is unsure about go to a muted Review track, and medium-confidence clips are marked [CHECK] for a quick look. The music engine finds beat drops faster and cuts on the kick or bass entry, and M4A/MP4 masters no longer cut about two frames late. Removing unused camera pieces is much faster on long sets, the backup copy before Apply is now an option (on by default), and the panel is tidier: Sync to Master Music and Podcast AI Director are on the main screen and the activity log moved to Help. Random Zoom keeps its v2.3.11.3 behaviour.

Also first public with this release: the panel reads the Camera Roles you type (shot size and direction) so two angles of the same kind never follow each other, four-beat cuts are kept at 25 and 30 fps, long DJ sets analyze much faster, a sequence In/Out range is respected when applying an AI plan, Stop and Continue work reliably, clips you disabled and muted camera tracks are never touched, and slow Premiere responses no longer leave buttons stuck. On Mac there are no more false low-memory pauses. The account keeps working offline for up to 72 hours, two open panels no longer sign each other out, and Premiere Pro 2022 (v22) and newer is supported. The update is optional (v2.3.11.2 and newer keep working): use Check Update in the panel or the website download, save and close Premiere before installing, and test on a duplicate project first.

v2.3.11.3 · PUBLIC · Windows & macOS · Safer installer

The installer now downloads one exact FFmpeg build (9.0.2) and checks its SHA-256 fingerprint before it is ever run. On Windows it uses the official GitHub release with gyan.dev as a backup source; on macOS it uses the matching Apple Silicon or Intel build from martin-riedl.de. If the file does not match, installation stops before your current plugin is changed. The first Windows install also downloads FFmpeg noticeably faster.

Customer packages no longer include internal training tools, the package configuration now matches the real checkout, and automatic pacing is now named Auto Music Map in the panel. The editing engine, Music Engine V18, Google/email login, trial dates and licenses are unchanged, and computers that already have FFmpeg see no difference. Use Check Update in the panel (or the website download): v2.3.11.2 users are asked to update on their next online check. Save and close Premiere before installing. Native Premiere testing on Windows and macOS hardware is still recommended on a duplicate project.

v2.3.11.2 · PUBLIC · Windows & macOS · Minimum 4-beat shots

Physical camera shots have a minimum duration of four beats. Optional short transitions are merged without moving exact drop timestamps. Cut pace below four beats is constrained by this minimum; pace 4/6/8 remains available. If coverage or closely spaced locked drops prevent a safe plan, Apply stops before timeline changes.

macOS retains automatic Low Memory mode on Macs with 12 GiB RAM or less: one CPU decoding thread, up to 24 optional visual samples, smaller timeline batches and memory preflight. Google/manual login, Music Engine V18, accounts and paid licenses are retained.

Save and quit Premiere before installing. Generate a fresh plan after updating; existing timelines are not automatically repaired and old resume jobs require regeneration. Test on a duplicate project. Automated regression and package checks pass; native Premiere and M1 memory behavior still require on-device testing. Minimum supported version is v2.3.11.2 for both OS, with zero grace period on the next online entitlement check.

v2.3.11.1 · PUBLIC · macOS only · Low Memory mode

Windows remains v2.3.11.0. On Macs with 12 GiB RAM or less, including M1 8 GB and Rosetta, optional AI Vision uses CPU frame decoding with one thread and at most 24 visual samples. Existing light timeline batches use 250 ms pacing. Analysis checks memory before starting and periodically while decoding audio; macOS uses estimated reclaimable VM pages rather than Windows commit thresholds.

Beat, BPM and drop resolution, Google/manual login, trial dates and lifetime licenses are retained. Automated tests, ZIP integrity and installer syntax pass; native M1/Premiere memory reduction is not yet measured. Save your project and quit Premiere before updating; test on a duplicate project. Proxies may still be necessary for large multicam projects. The existing zero-grace-period minimum applies per OS: macOS v2.3.11.1, Windows v2.3.11.0.

v2.3.11.0 · Windows PUBLIC / previous macOS · Google login in the plugin

Continue with Google is now included in the Windows and macOS Premiere panels, with a local Google icon, automatic browser-to-plugin sign-in, cancellation and timeout handling. Email/password login remains available. PKCE protects the code exchange; the plugin verifies the account and existing license/device rules before enabling access. Trial dates and paid licenses are not reset.

Music Engine V18 is unchanged. The numeric version jump lets .137 TEST and PUBLIC installations receive this update through Check Update. Windows installer and rollback are tested in isolated folders; macOS uses syntax and mocked checks, not native Premiere validation. Final Google account consent must be tested on your device.

v2.3.10.137 · Previous PUBLIC · Music Engine V18

Multi-signal BPM and beat tracking combines broad onset, bass attacks and rhythmic transient/flux. Tempo continuity helps ambiguous or weak-signal regions; onset backtracking refines drop timing. Balanced UI default spacing is 64 beats; saved settings and camera-cut cadence are preserved.

Optional local label export, model import and benchmark tools are included. No trained model is bundled; the default remains rules-only. A matched-setting comparison on one 46-second recording showed steadier tempo estimates, not proof of accuracy on all music.

Save your project, close Adobe apps and install the public ZIP. Run Analyze Music again and generate a fresh plan; TEST and older music caches are not reused. Existing .137 TEST users must manually reinstall because the numeric version is the same. Windows installation/rollback passed isolated tests; macOS installer and updater passed syntax and mocked-filesystem checks. Native Premiere and native macOS validation are still required.

The online minimum version now follows the public release without a grace period. Older versions lose access on the next online entitlement check; accounts and valid licenses are retained. Both packages include verified auto-update helpers and manual installers.

v2.3.10.136 · Windows & macOS · Music Engine V17

New kick-weighted BPM estimation, fractional beat-grid timing, all-beat drop candidates, tempo-relative analysis windows and precise onset refinement. Run Analyze Music again after updating. Existing cut cadence and Balanced UI 96-beat drop spacing are unchanged.

Midtrans checkout is restored in the plugin. Review the server-quoted IDR amount before opening checkout, then use Check Payment after paying. Updater and account improvements from .133 are retained. Payment methods depend on merchant activation.

  • Guided first-edit checklist inside the plugin.
  • Wedding, concert and event pacing presets using existing engine settings.
  • Quick access to camera-plan review, explanations and export.
  • Website, help center and local diagnostic summary links.
  • Existing 7-day server-timed trial and account-based lifetime licensing retained.

Music analysis uses the V17 onset cache and updated tempo/drop detection; the V82-FAST editing core is retained. Podcast speaker tracking remains unavailable. Packages receive static validation; Premiere runtime testing on Windows and macOS is still required.
