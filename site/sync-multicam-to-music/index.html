Sync multicam footage to the master music in Premiere Pro

By Rizki Ardiansyah, creator of R Project Multicam AI · Updated 18 October 2026

Before any camera can switch on the beat, every clip has to sit at the right moment against the music. Sync Cameras to Music, step 2 in the Multicam AI panel, lines up every camera, drone and FPV clip on the master mix on A1, so you can go straight to music-driven multicam editing.

What Sync Cameras to Music does

Live events rarely give you one continuous file per camera. Cameras are stopped and restarted, phones record short clips and batteries are swapped mid-set. Lining those clips up by hand against a long DJ mix can take hours.

Sync Cameras to Music reads the master music on A1, listens to each camera clip's own audio and places the clip where it belongs on the timeline. It also uses the camera clock to keep clips from the same camera in the right order. The result is a new synced sequence; your current sequence is not changed. Skip this step if your cameras are already in sync.

Drone and FPV clips

Drone and FPV clips often have no usable audio. They are placed from their camera clock instead, bridged through a camera that synced by audio, and land on their own [CLOCK] track. If a drone is off, move one of its clips to the right spot, select it and click Use selected clip. Every clip of that drone follows. The correction is remembered per drone and shoot date, so the next project from the same shoot needs no fix. Gyroflow FPV exports are remembered by their bin name.

When to use it

  • DJ sets and club nights: several cameras with many start/stop clips and one clean master recording from the mixer.
  • Concerts and festivals: stage, crowd, handheld, drone and FPV angles recorded separately from the board mix.
  • Short clips: very short clips are placed too; clips that are too short or too unclear to place reliably go to Review instead of stopping the job.

How confidence and review work

Every placement gets a confidence check. Clips the engine is sure about are placed directly. Clips to look at are labelled [CHECK] so you can jump to them and confirm the position in a few seconds. Clips it is unsure about are put on a muted Review track, so nothing questionable ends up in the edit without you seeing it.

Step by step in Premiere Pro

  1. Open a sequence with the master music or the mixer recording on A1.
  2. Import the camera clips. Put each camera in its own bin, or each camera on its own video track with the clips placed roughly. Drone and FPV clips without audio go in their own bins.
  3. In the Multicam AI panel, go to step 2 · Sync Cameras to Music.
  4. Click 1. Find camera sources and tick one bin (or one video track) per camera.
  5. Click 2. Analyze sync, then 3. Build synced sequence.
  6. Open the new synced sequence. Check the [CHECK] clips, the muted Review track and the [CLOCK] track, then continue with music-driven multicam editing.

Tips for the best result

  • Record camera audio for every angle, even if you will not use it in the final mix: the camera audio is what the sync listens to.
  • Use the cleanest master you have, ideally a direct recording from the mixer.
  • Set the camera and drone clocks before the event; drone and FPV clips are placed by their clock.
  • Keep a backup of your project before working on a long set, and review the synced sequence before you start cutting.

Sync Cameras to Music questions

Does Sync Cameras to Music change my original sequence?

No. It builds a new synced sequence and leaves your current sequence as it is.

Which license do I need?

Sync Cameras to Music is for the lifetime license only. It is not part of the 7-day trial, which includes steps 1, 3, 4 and 5. It is a beta feature in the current plugin releases.

What happens to clips that cannot be matched?

Clips the engine is unsure about are placed on a muted Review track, and clips to look at are labelled [CHECK]. Nothing is deleted.

How do I fix a drone clip that is off?

Move one clip of that drone to the right spot, select it and click Use selected clip. All clips of that drone follow, and the correction is remembered for that drone and shoot date.

Do I still need to review the result?

Yes. This is a beta feature: check the [CHECK] clips, the Review track and the [CLOCK] track before you edit. Camera audio quality, crowd noise and very short clips affect how reliably a clip can be placed.

Next: let the music drive the cut

Once the cameras are in sync, click Analyze Music in step 1, then Run Auto Cut in step 3 · Auto Cut Multicam. The camera plan follows beats, phrases and drops. Camera roles keep the edit varied: two angles of the same kind, such as wide after wide, never follow each other. See the AI multicam editor for Premiere Pro and the music-driven editing guide.
