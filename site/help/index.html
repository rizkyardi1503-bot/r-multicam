Three steps to your first edit

1. Install

Save your project, close Premiere, extract the Windows or macOS release and run the included installer.

Installation guide

2. Prepare

Put each camera on its own V track and your music on A1. Work on a duplicate sequence. Set In/Out if you want to process a specific range. Cameras not in sync? Use step 2 · Sync Cameras to Music (lifetime license).

3. Review & apply

Follow the numbered steps in the panel. Click Analyze Music in step 1. In step 3 · Auto Cut Multicam, choose the pace and camera roles, preview the camera plan if you like, then click Run Auto Cut.

Editing guide

Premiere Pro extension not showing?

Close Premiere before installing and restart it when installation finishes. Look under Window → Extensions (Legacy) → R Project Multicam AI. Check the release ZIP’s instructions and compatibility requirements for your operating system and Premiere version. If the panel still does not appear, contact support with those details and the installer error.

Accounts, trial and payments

How does the 7-day trial start?

Create and confirm your account, then sign in inside the plugin. First plugin activation starts the server-timed trial. Reinstalling or changing devices does not restart that account’s trial. Creating an account on the website alone does not start it.

I paid, but my license still shows trial

Open My license on the website, sign in with the account used at checkout and select Check payment. After payment is verified, refresh your license inside Premiere. Pending payments do not activate lifetime access.

I changed computers

Sign in to the plugin on the new computer. One device can be active per account; the latest sign-in replaces the previous plugin session. Website sign-in does not replace your active plugin device.

Editing and troubleshooting

No music or beats detected

Confirm that the sequence contains readable music on A1 and that the selected In/Out range covers it. Check FFmpeg under Advanced → Settings in the panel. Try clearer music, then click Analyze Music again.

A camera does not appear in the edit

Check Camera roles & cameras used and the footage coverage of each track. Where a chosen camera has no footage, the edit uses another enabled camera. If a camera’s footage ends inside a shot, the edit switches to the camera that continues. Run Auto Cut again after changing footage.

How do I inspect a plan before applying?

In step 3 · Auto Cut Multicam, open Preview camera plan before applying. Click Analyze Music first, then generate the plan and check the camera choices and reasons. Nothing changes on the timeline until you apply. Export Plan JSON if you need a detailed record. Apply · Keep Clips keeps the unused camera pieces.

My cameras are not in sync yet

Use step 2 · Sync Cameras to Music (beta, lifetime license only; not in the 7-day trial). Click 1. Find camera sources and tick one bin per camera, or one video track per camera. Put drone and FPV clips without audio in their own bins. Then click 2. Analyze sync and 3. Build synced sequence. Clips to look at are marked [CHECK]; unsure clips go to a muted Review track. A new sequence is built; your current one is not changed. How Sync Cameras to Music works.

Auto Cut stopped before it finished

Open Continue Multicam in step 3 to resume the saved job. Do not change the sequence or A1 before you resume.

Which presets should I choose?

Pick an Editing preset in step 3. Wedding uses slower 6/8-beat pacing, Concert uses 2/4 beats and Event uses 4/6/8 beats. Cut Pace counts real beats, and a drop restarts the count. The shortest shot is 4 beats, so 2 Beat gives cuts of 4 to 6 beats. Review the result. Podcast AI Director is still Coming Soon.

How do updates work?

Select Check Update in the plugin. Read the release notes and save your project. Windows can prepare a verified update; close Premiere before installation. Follow the release’s macOS installer instructions.
