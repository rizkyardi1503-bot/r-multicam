Automatic beat detection for Premiere Pro

By Rizki Ardiansyah, creator of R Project Multicam AI · Updated 25 October 2026

Premiere Pro can play music and show its waveform, but it does not find the beats for you. R Project Multicam AI is a Premiere Pro plugin for Windows and macOS that analyzes the music on A1, detects BPM, beats, build-ups and the main drops, and uses that map to cut synced multicam footage on the beat.

Does Premiere Pro detect beats? The native method

Premiere Pro has no built-in automatic beat detection. The usual method is to mark the beats by hand:

  1. Click an empty spot in the timeline so no clip is selected, so M adds sequence markers.
  2. Play the sequence and press M on every beat you want to use, or only on the first beat of every bar.
  3. Zoom in on the waveform and drag markers that landed early or late onto the kick drum peaks.
  4. Turn on snapping and cut on the markers, or switch multicam angles near them.

That works for a 30-second reel. On a one-hour DJ set it means hundreds of markers before the first cut. The full manual workflow is in how to cut to the beat in Premiere Pro.

What the plugin detects

Click Analyze Music in step 1 of the panel. The plugin reads the music on A1, or the In/Out range you set, and builds a music map:

  • Beats and BPM: the beat grid and the tempo of the track or set.
  • Phrases and energy: energy changes, build-ups and breakdowns.
  • Drops: main-drop candidates with a drop score. Open Choose the main drops if you want to pick them yourself.

The panel shows BPM, Drops, Energy and Drop score so you can check the analysis before cutting. The analysis runs locally in the plugin using FFmpeg/DSP. No online AI service or API key is needed; internet is still required for account login, licensing and updates.

How the cuts use the beat map

You do not have to place markers first: step 3 · Auto Cut Multicam uses the music map directly to plan camera switches across the video tracks you enable.

  • Cut Pace counts real beats: choose 2, 4, 6 or 8 beats, or allow several intervals for music-aware pacing. Faster music gets denser cuts at the same pace.
  • Drops restart the count: a validated main drop forces a camera change where the music hits, and the cadence continues from there. A Main Drop Camera gives that moment a chosen angle.
  • Shortest shot: 4 beats, so 2 Beat gives cuts of 4 to 6 beats.
  • Camera roles: two angles of the same kind, such as wide after wide, never follow each other. A physical cut is only made when the camera changes.
  • Best Drops: step 4 finds the strongest drops and renders short highlight clips around them.

Open Preview camera plan to check the plan before anything changes on the timeline. The result is a normal Premiere Pro timeline that you can keep editing by hand.

How to run beat detection in Premiere Pro

  1. Install the plugin, open Premiere Pro and open the panel under Window → Extensions (Legacy) → R Project Multicam AI.
  2. Put the music or mixer recording on A1 and each camera on its own video track. Work on a duplicate sequence and set In/Out for a short test range.
  3. If the cameras are not in sync yet, run step 2 · Sync Cameras to Music (beta, lifetime license only).
  4. Click Analyze Music and check BPM and the detected drops.
  5. In step 3 · Auto Cut Multicam, choose the Cut Pace and camera roles, open Preview camera plan, then click Run Auto Cut.
  6. Watch the whole result and adjust cuts by hand where the performance needs it.

Who it is for

  • DJ sets and club nights: long mixes where cuts should follow beats, phrases and drops.
  • Concerts and festivals: stage, crowd, handheld and drone angles cut to the board mix.
  • Event recaps and weddings: presets such as Event (4/6/8 beats) and Wedding (slower 6/8 beats) as a starting point.

It is not built for podcasts or interviews: those need speaker-driven switching, and Podcast AI editing is still coming soon.

Limits: what to check yourself

  • Detection needs review and there is no accuracy guarantee. Results depend on the recording, the detected music timing and the camera coverage.
  • If no music or beats are detected, check that A1 has readable music inside the In/Out range and that FFmpeg is set under Advanced → Settings, then try clearer music.
  • Beat timing alone does not decide which shot tells the story. Hold important performance moments by hand.
  • Cuts only line up when every camera is in sync with the music on A1.
  • Adobe Premiere Pro 2022 (v22) or newer is required and sold separately.

Beat detection in Premiere Pro: questions

Does Premiere Pro have built-in beat detection?

No. Premiere Pro does not add beat markers automatically. The native method is to play the sequence and press M on the beats, then adjust the markers by hand.

Does beat detection need an internet connection or online AI?

The music analysis runs locally in the plugin using FFmpeg/DSP, with no online AI service or API key. Internet is still needed for account login, licensing and updates.

Is beat detection included in the free trial?

Yes. The 7-day trial includes Analyze Music, Auto Cut Multicam, Best Drops and Random Zoom. It starts when you first activate the plugin in Premiere and needs no payment card. Sync Cameras to Music needs the lifetime license.

How accurate is the beat and drop detection?

There is no accuracy guarantee. Results depend on the recording, so check BPM and drops after Analyze Music, pick the main drops yourself if needed and review the edit before delivery.

Which Premiere Pro versions are supported?

Adobe Premiere Pro 2022 (v22) or newer on Windows or macOS. Premiere Pro is required and sold separately.
