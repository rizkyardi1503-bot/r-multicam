THE PRODUCT

Every cut starts with the music.

Beat analysis, camera control and editing tools inside Premiere Pro.

AUTO MUSIC MAP V14

Read the structure.
Follow the music.

Analyze the full A1 music track once using local FFmpeg/DSP analysis. Beat, BPM, phrase, energy, build-up, breakdown and drop impact inform the camera plan.

Main drops are selected using confidence, structural evidence and spacing. Multicam and Best Music Drops reuse the ranked music map when the sequence and A1 are unchanged.

BEATDROP
V82-FAST EDITING ENGINE

Cut when the camera changes.

Beat-aligned decision points guide the edit, but a physical razor cut is made only when the selected camera changes. Same-camera decisions stay continuous. The workflow creates no Premiere timeline markers.

2 / 4 / 6 / 8 BEATS

Choose your cut pace.

Select one interval for fixed pacing, or several to let the music map choose among them. Validated main drops interrupt the cadence, switch the angle and restart the beat count.

CAMERA ROLES & COVERAGE

Use the cameras that fit.

Set camera roles and enable the tracks you want. When a preferred camera has no footage coverage, the director falls back to another enabled camera that covers the interval. If none does, the original footage is preserved.

MAIN DROP CAMERA

Give the impact its angle.

Choose a video track for validated main-drop impacts, or leave it on Auto. Your chosen camera is used when enabled and covered; otherwise the director selects a covered alternative.

RANDOM ZOOM / ACTIVE SCENES

Add movement after the edit.

Set minimum and maximum Scale values. Each compatible clip receives two Scale keyframes with a randomly chosen zoom-in or zoom-out direction.

Run after multicam cleanup completes. Existing Scale keyframes in the processed source range are replaced; unsupported clips and locked or muted tracks are skipped.

BEST MUSIC DROPS

Turn top drops into exports.

Analyze first, review the valid drop count, then choose how many top-ranked drops to render. Adjust sensitivity and beat-based pre/post windows; duration follows the music's BPM.

H.264 Match Source exports are saved beside your Premiere project in a Best Music Drops folder. Choose an H.264 preset manually if automatic detection cannot find one.

Contact WhatsApp, rizkyardi1503@gmail.com or message @r.project__ on Instagram with your operating system, Premiere version and the installation step or error message. Never send your password or API keys.

Does this work inside Premiere Pro?

Yes. Multicam AI is a Premiere Pro extension. Prepare your sequence in Premiere and use the panel to analyze music and apply camera switches.

Do I need an online AI subscription?

Local music analysis is available. Online AI review is optional and requires your own supported API key. Provider charges are separate from the plugin license.
