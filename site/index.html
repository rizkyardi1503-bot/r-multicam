Which Premiere versions and systems are supported?

Adobe Premiere Pro 2022 (v22) and newer on Windows and macOS. Public installers are available for both; check release-specific compatibility for your Premiere version before installing.

Can it sync my camera clips to the master music?

Yes. Sync Cameras to Music (beta) lines up every camera, drone and FPV clip on the master music on A1 and builds a new synced sequence. Camera clips are placed by their own audio; drone and FPV clips by their camera clock. Uncertain clips go to a muted Review track.

Do I need an online AI subscription?

No. Music analysis runs locally in the plugin. You only need internet for sign-in, license checks and updates.

How much does it cost?

Monthly is Rp99.000 per 30 days, with a founder price of Rp59.000 for the first 100 subscribers. Annual is Rp799.000 per 365 days, 33% less than 12 standard monthly plans. A lifetime license (Rp2.000.000) is sold until 30 November 2026. Prices are in IDR, the same in every country, and the final amount is shown before payment.

Does it renew automatically?

No. Plans are prepaid through Midtrans and never charge you again by themselves. To stop, simply do not renew. If you pay before a plan ends, the new days are added on top of the days you have left.

What happens when my plan ends?

The license shows as expired and access pauses until you pay for another plan. Your Premiere projects and files are not changed. Paying again starts a new period right away.

What is the founder price?

The first 100 subscribers pay Rp59.000 instead of Rp99.000 for the Monthly plan. They keep it while they renew before the plan ends or within 7 days after it ends. After that gap the standard price applies. Affiliate codes cannot be combined with the founder price.

What happens to lifetime?

Lifetime licenses are sold until 30 November 2026 (23:59 WITA). Everyone who already owns a lifetime license keeps it, with no renewals. After that date new customers choose Monthly or Annual.
