HOW IT WORKS

A clear path to your next edit.

The whole workflow in 67 seconds, with the real Premiere Pro panel and a real Druva set. On-screen captions are in Indonesian.

CHAPTERS · 1:07

  1. 01

    Prepare your footage.

    Each camera on its own video track, the music on A1. Use the full sequence or set In/Out. Cameras not in sync? Sync Cameras to Music lines them up.

  2. 02

    Analyze the music.

    Analyze Music reads tempo, beats and drops locally with FFmpeg/DSP and shows BPM, Drops, Energy and Drop score.

  3. 03

    Set the pace.

    Choose a Cut Pace of 2, 4, 6 or 8 beats, your camera roles and a Main Drop Camera. Preview the plan if you like.

  4. 04

    Apply and keep creating.

    Run Auto Cut to switch cameras on the beat, then add Best Drops and Random Zoom.

Does R Project Multicam AI work inside Adobe Premiere Pro?

Yes. It is a Premiere Pro extension. Prepare synchronized camera tracks and music in Premiere, then use the panel to analyze the music and apply camera switches.

Which Premiere versions and systems are supported?

Adobe Premiere Pro 2022 (v22) and newer on Windows and macOS. Public installers are available for both; check release-specific compatibility for your Premiere version before installing.

Can it sync my camera clips to the master music?

Yes. Sync Cameras to Music (beta, lifetime license only) lines up every camera, drone and FPV clip on the master music on A1 and builds a new synced sequence. Camera clips are placed by their own audio; drone and FPV clips by their camera clock. Uncertain clips go to a muted Review track.

Can the plugin detect beats, BPM and music drops?

Yes. Local FFmpeg and DSP analysis identifies beat timing, BPM, music structure, energy changes and main-drop candidates used by the camera plan.

Is it useful for DJ sets and live events?

Yes. The workflow is designed for music-driven multicamera footage such as DJ sets, concerts, festivals and live-event edits where camera changes need to follow the rhythm.

Do I need an online AI subscription?

No. Music analysis runs locally in the plugin. You only need internet for sign-in, license checks and updates.

How much is the lifetime license?

The lifetime license is Rp2.000.000 as a one-time purchase, with no subscription. The final IDR amount is shown before payment.

How do I activate my license?

Use the same account for your purchase and the plugin. After the payment has been verified and the license assigned, sign in inside Premiere to check your access.

Can I sign in with Google?

Yes. Google sign-in is available on the website and in plugin v2.3.11.2 or newer. Email/password sign-in remains available for the same account.

Does it work in DaVinci Resolve?

Yes, on Windows. The DaVinci Resolve version (v1.0.0, tested with DaVinci Resolve 21 Free and Studio) uses the same account, license and music engine. Run Install-DaVinci.bat, then choose Workspace → Scripts → R Project Multicam AI. It builds a new multicam timeline and renders Best Drops; the original timeline is not changed. DaVinci Resolve on macOS is not supported yet.

Is the result ready without review?

Review the result before delivery. Camera selection, footage coverage and music style affect the edit; you can refine the timeline in Premiere.
