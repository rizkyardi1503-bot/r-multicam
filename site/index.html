How Sync Cameras to Music works ↗
V82-FAST EDITING ENGINE

Cut when the camera changes.

Beat-aligned decision points guide the edit, but a physical razor cut is made only when the selected camera changes. Same-camera decisions stay continuous. The workflow creates no Premiere timeline markers.

2 / 4 / 6 / 8 BEATS

Choose your cut pace.

Select one interval for fixed pacing, or several to let the music map choose among them. Validated main drops interrupt the cadence, switch the angle and restart the beat count.

CAMERA ROLES & CONTRAST

Never wide after wide.

Describe each camera in plain words (for example “Wide Crowd”, “Left Side DJ”, “DJ Deck Close”). The director reads shot size and direction from each role and never cuts between two angles of the same kind, so a wide shot is always followed by a different view.

When a preferred camera has no footage coverage, the director falls back to another enabled camera that covers the interval. If none does, the original footage is preserved.

MAIN DROP CAMERA

Give the impact its angle.

Choose a video track for validated main-drop impacts, or leave it on Auto. Your chosen camera is used when enabled and covered; otherwise the director selects a covered alternative.

RANDOM ZOOM / ACTIVE SCENES

Add movement after the edit.

Set minimum and maximum Scale values. Each compatible clip receives two Scale keyframes with a randomly chosen zoom-in or zoom-out direction.

Run after multicam cleanup completes. Existing Scale keyframes in the processed source range are replaced; unsupported clips and locked or muted tracks are skipped.

BEST MUSIC DROPS

Turn top drops into exports.

Analyze first, review the valid drop count, then choose how many top-ranked drops to render. Adjust sensitivity and beat-based pre/post windows; duration follows the music's BPM.

H.264 Match Source exports are saved beside your Premiere project in a Best Music Drops folder. Choose an H.264 preset manually if automatic detection cannot find one.

SAFE APPLY & CONTINUE

Edit long sets with confidence.

A backup copy of your sequence can be saved before Apply (on by default). Sequence In/Out is respected, clips you disabled stay disabled, muted tracks are never touched, and Stop / Continue picks up where the job left off.

The license keeps working offline for up to 72 hours after the last online check. Supports Premiere Pro 2022 (v22) and newer on Windows and macOS.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

A few things
before you start.

Who can help me install or activate the plugin?

Contact WhatsApp, rizkyardi1503@gmail.com or message @r.project__ on Instagram with your operating system, Premiere version and the installation step or error message. Never send your password or API keys.

Can it sync my camera clips to the master music?

Yes. Sync Cameras to Music (beta, lifetime license only) lines up every camera, drone and FPV clip on the master music on A1 and builds a new synced sequence. Camera clips are placed by their own audio; drone and FPV clips by their camera clock. Uncertain clips go to a muted Review track.

Does this work inside Premiere Pro?

Yes. Multicam AI is a Premiere Pro extension. Prepare your sequence in Premiere and use the panel to analyze music and apply camera switches.

Do I need an online AI subscription?

No. Music analysis runs locally in the plugin. You only need internet for sign-in, license checks and updates.

How do I activate my license?

Use the same account for your purchase and the plugin. After the payment has been verified and the license assigned, sign in inside Premiere to check your access.

Is the result ready without review?

Review the result before delivery. Camera selection, footage coverage and music style affect the edit; you can refine the timeline in Premiere.

Which Premiere versions are supported?

Multicam AI supports Adobe Premiere Pro 2022 (v22) and newer on Windows and macOS. Installation and runtime compatibility should be confirmed for your specific Premiere version and operating system.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST EDIT

Premiere Pro plugin questions, answered.

Does R Project Multicam AI work inside Adobe Premiere Pro?

Yes. R Project Multicam AI is a Premiere Pro extension. Prepare synchronized camera tracks and music in Premiere, then use the panel to analyze the music and apply camera switches.

Can the plugin detect beats, BPM and music drops?

Yes. Local FFmpeg and DSP analysis can identify beat timing, BPM, music structure, energy changes and main-drop candidates used by the camera plan.

Is R Project Multicam AI useful for DJ sets and live events?

Yes. The workflow is designed for music-driven multicamera footage such as DJ sets, concerts, festivals and live-event edits where camera changes need to follow the rhythm.

Does the plugin support Windows and macOS?

Yes. Public installers are available for Windows and macOS. Check release-specific Premiere Pro compatibility before installing.
