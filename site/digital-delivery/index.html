PURCHASE / DIGITAL DELIVERY

Digital delivery & purchase process

R Project Multicam AI is a digital software product. No physical goods are shipped.

On this page

  1. Free trial delivery
  2. Lifetime purchase
  3. Delivery timing

Free trial delivery

  1. Create an R Project account on this website.
  2. Confirm your email and sign in.
  3. Choose Windows or macOS and download the installer.
  4. Install the plugin in Adobe Premiere Pro and sign in.
  5. Your server-timed 7-day trial starts on the first plugin activation.

Lifetime purchase

  1. Sign in with the same R Project account used in the plugin.
  2. Select Lifetime License. The standard price is Rp2.000.000 one-time.
  3. Review the final IDR amount and continue to Midtrans checkout when the payment gateway is available.
  4. Complete payment using an available Midtrans payment method.
  5. Midtrans sends payment confirmation to R Project.
  6. After verification, the account license changes to Lifetime.
  7. Refresh the license in the Premiere plugin or sign in again to use lifetime access.

Delivery timing

Installer access is available after account confirmation and sign-in. Lifetime activation is normally electronic after successful payment verification. Payment verification timing depends on the payment method and provider. A pending payment does not activate lifetime access. If payment is marked successful but your license has not updated, click Check payment in My account, then refresh the license in the plugin. If it still does not update, contact rizkyardi1503@gmail.com or WhatsApp support with your account email, order ID and payment confirmation. Do not make a second payment to resolve an activation issue.

What the customer receives

The customer receives account-based access to the R Project Multicam AI installer, the 7-day trial where eligible, and lifetime account activation after a verified lifetime purchase. Installation guides, release notes and support links are available on this website.
