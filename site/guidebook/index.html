Multicam AI quick guide: see how it works first

By Rizki Ardiansyah, creator of R Project Multicam AI · Updated 8 November 2026

What you need

  • Adobe Premiere Pro on a Windows or Mac laptop or desktop. Step 6 · Color LUT needs Premiere Pro 2026.
  • Footage from each camera and one music file: the master track or the recording from the mixer.
  • A free R Project account. The 7-day trial needs no payment card.

Step A · Install and sign in

Sign in on the website and download the ZIP for Windows or Mac. Close Premiere, extract the ZIP and run SETUP-WINDOWS.cmd (Windows) or Install-macOS.command (Mac).

Open Premiere, choose Window → Extensions (Legacy) → R Project Multicam AI and sign in with the same account. The 7-day trial starts the first time you sign in to the plugin.

The panel: six numbered steps

Work from top to bottom. Step 1 and step 3 make the edit; steps 2, 4, 5 and 6 are optional. The 7-day trial and the lifetime license open every step.

Before you start: save your project and duplicate the sequence. Put each camera on its own video track and the music on A1. For a first test, set a short In/Out range.

Step 1 · Analyze Music

Click Analyze Music. The plugin reads the tempo, beats and drops of the music on A1 and shows BPM, Drops, Energy and Drop score. Open Choose the main drops if you want to pick the main drops yourself.

Step 2 · Sync Cameras to Music (beta, optional)

Skip this step if your cameras are already in sync. Otherwise click 1. Find camera sources and tick one bin (or one video track) per camera, then 2. Analyze sync and 3. Build synced sequence. The plugin builds a new synced sequence; your current sequence is not changed. Check the clips labelled [CHECK], the muted Review track and the [CLOCK] track for drones. Since v2.3.11.19, clips placed from another camera’s audio are also marked [CHECK], audio-only files are left out and network paths work.

Step 3 · Auto Cut Multicam

Pick an Editing preset or set the Cut Pace (2, 4, 6 or 8 beats). Choose the Main Drop Camera and the cameras to use under Camera roles & cameras used. Open Preview camera plan before applying to check the plan first. Keep Save backup copy of sequence before applying ticked, then click Run Auto Cut.

Step 4 · Best Drops (optional)

Click Find Best Drops to find the strongest drops in the music. Set how long each clip runs with Before drop and After drop, check them under Review ranked drops, then render them: Adobe Media Encoder exports short highlight clips into a Best Music Drops folder next to your project.

Step 5 · Random Zoom (optional)

After Auto Cut has finished, click Add Random Zoom to add zoom moves to the clips of the edited sequence. Set the zoom range (100–115% by default) under Zoom settings.

Step 6 · Color LUT (new)

Pick a LUT from the list or click Add .cube LUT…, set the Intensity, choose Apply to (all footage in the active sequence or the selected clips) and click Apply LUT. Run it again to change the LUT or the intensity: the same effect is updated, nothing stacks. Remove LUT deletes only the plugin’s own effect, so your own Lumetri grade stays safe. Needs Premiere Pro 2026.

Safe for your project

  • Sync Cameras to Music builds a new sequence; your original sequence is not changed.
  • Auto Cut saves a backup copy of the sequence before applying (on by default).
  • Remove LUT deletes only the LUT effect the plugin added.
  • The trial needs no card. One account is active on one device at a time.

Ready to try it?

Create a free account and download on your laptop or desktop. If you keep using the plugin after the trial, the lifetime license is a one-time payment.
