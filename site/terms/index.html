Product

R Project Multicam AI is digital software for Adobe Premiere Pro. It assists with music analysis and multicamera editing workflows. Adobe Premiere Pro is required and sold separately.

Trial

A confirmed account can download the installer. The 7-day server-timed trial starts on the first plugin activation, not when the website account is created. Reinstalling or changing devices does not restart the trial.
