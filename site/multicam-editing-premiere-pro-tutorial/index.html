How to edit multicam in Premiere Pro

By Rizki Ardiansyah, creator of R Project Multicam AI · Updated 28 October 2026

Multicam editing lets you watch several synced camera angles at once and switch between them like a live director. This tutorial walks through the native Premiere Pro workflow step by step, covers the problems that stop multicam from working, and shows an automatic way to cut music-driven footage such as DJ sets, concerts and events.

Before you start: footage and project

  • Record usable audio on every camera, even if you will not use it in the final mix. Premiere Pro syncs by comparing that audio.
  • For music edits, have the cleanest music source ready: the mastered track, or a direct recording from the mixing desk.
  • Put the clips of each camera in their own bin and give the bins clear names, such as Wide, Close-up and Crowd.
  • Save the project and work on a copy of the sequence, so you can always go back.

Manual: edit multicam in Premiere Pro step by step

1. Create a multi-camera source sequence

  1. In the Project panel, select the clips of all cameras plus the music or mixer recording.
  2. Right-click the selection and choose Create Multi-Camera Source Sequence.
  3. Choose the sync point: Audio for most event footage, Timecode if all cameras shared timecode, or In points or clip markers if you marked a visible sync moment such as a clap or flash.
  4. Pick the audio setting that keeps the music you want to hear, then confirm. The new multicam source sequence appears in the Project panel.

2. Put the multicam clip in a sequence

Right-click the multicam source sequence and create a new sequence from it, or drag it into an existing sequence. If it lands as separate clips instead of one clip, switch the timeline setting for inserting sequences to nests and drag it in again.

Another way: sync on the timeline and nest

Put every camera on its own video track, select the clips, right-click and choose Synchronize, then sync by audio. Select the synced clips, right-click and choose Nest. Finally, right-click the nested sequence and enable Multi-Camera.

3. Turn on Multi-Camera view

In the Program Monitor, open the settings menu (wrench icon) and switch to Multi-Camera view, or add the Toggle Multi-Camera View button through the Button Editor. All angles appear on the left and the active angle on the right. Make sure the track with the multicam clip is targeted.

4. Switch angles while it plays

Play the sequence and press the number keys (1, 2, 3 …) or click an angle to switch cameras. When you stop playback, every switch is a cut in the timeline. Do a first pass for the rough rhythm and do not stop for every small mistake.

5. Refine the cuts

  • Move a cut earlier or later with the Rolling Edit tool (N) without changing the length of the sequence.
  • Change the angle of one segment: stop playback, put the playhead on the segment and click another angle, or right-click the clip and pick a camera from its Multi-Camera menu.
  • For music, snap the cuts to the beat. See how to cut to the beat in Premiere Pro for beat markers and phrasing.

6. Flatten when the edit is final

When every angle choice is final, select the multicam clips, right-click and choose Multi-Camera, then Flatten. The clips are replaced by the original camera clips, which is handy before color grading or handing over the project. After flattening you can no longer switch angles on those clips without undoing it.

Multicam in Premiere Pro not working? Common problems and fixes

Audio sync fails or puts clips in the wrong place

Audio sync compares waveforms, so it struggles with cameras that recorded almost no sound, heavy crowd noise or long gaps. Check that every clip really has audio, sync a few cameras at a time, or mark a clear sync moment by hand and sync by In points or clip markers. Cameras that were stopped and restarted many times are the slowest to line up by hand.

A drone or FPV clip has no audio to sync

Without audio there is nothing for audio sync to compare. Use timecode if the drone shared it, mark a visible moment that other cameras also show, or place the clip by hand against the music. Sync Cameras to Music (beta, lifetime license) places drone and FPV clips from their camera clock, bridged through a camera that synced by audio, on their own [CLOCK] track.

The multicam option or Multi-Camera view does nothing

Create Multi-Camera Source Sequence works on clips selected in the Project panel. In the timeline, the clip must be a multicam source sequence or a nested sequence with Multi-Camera enabled, and its track must be targeted. A sequence that was dropped in as separate clips does not switch angles.

The angles are in the wrong order

The angle numbers follow the order of the cameras in the multicam source sequence. Open the source sequence and move the camera tracks into the order you want, or reorder and disable cameras from the Multi-Camera options in the Program Monitor settings menu.

Playback lags in Multi-Camera view

Multi-Camera view decodes every angle at once. Lower the playback resolution in the Program Monitor to 1/2 or 1/4, create proxies for heavy camera files and turn proxies on, and disable angles you do not need. On a low-memory laptop, test on a short duplicate sequence first.

The automatic way: music-driven multicam with R Project Multicam AI

Switching angles by hand works well for a short clip. On a one-hour DJ set or concert it means hundreds of decisions. R Project Multicam AI is a Premiere Pro panel for Windows and macOS that plans the camera cuts from the music. The music analysis runs locally in the plugin.

  1. Put each camera on its own video track and the music on A1. Work on a duplicate sequence and set In/Out to process only part of it.
  2. If the cameras are not in sync, run step 2 · Sync Cameras to Music first (beta, lifetime license only). It builds a new synced sequence and leaves your current sequence as it is. On the 7-day trial, or when the cameras are already in sync, skip step 2 and go straight to step 1 and step 3.
  3. In step 1, click Analyze Music. The plugin detects beats, BPM, phrases and drops on A1.
  4. In step 3 · Auto Cut Multicam, choose a Cut Pace of 2, 4, 6 or 8 beats, set the camera roles (for example wide, close-up and crowd) and choose the cameras to use.
  5. Open Preview camera plan to check the plan, then click Run Auto Cut and review the result.

The result is a normal Premiere Pro timeline, so every cut can still be trimmed, moved or replaced by hand. Detection needs review and there is no accuracy guarantee, so check the whole edit before delivery. Sync Cameras to Music needs the lifetime license, Rp2.000.000 one-time.

Manual or automatic: which should you choose?

  • Manual suits interviews, dialogue, story-driven edits and short pieces where you want to pick every angle yourself.
  • Automatic suits long music recordings such as DJ sets, concerts, festivals and event recaps, where the cuts should follow beats, phrases and drops.
  • Both: run the automatic pass first, then refine key moments by hand with the steps above.

Multicam in Premiere Pro: questions

Why is multicam not working in Premiere Pro?

Usually the clip in the timeline is not a multicam clip: it was dropped in as individual clips, Multi-Camera is not enabled on the nested sequence, or its track is not targeted. Check those three things, then turn on Multi-Camera view in the Program Monitor.

How do I sync a camera that has no audio, such as a drone?

Audio sync needs audio on every clip, so a silent drone clip has to be placed by timecode, by a visible sync point marked with In points or clip markers, or by hand. Sync Cameras to Music (beta, lifetime license) places drone and FPV clips from their camera clock.

Can Premiere Pro switch multicam angles automatically?

Premiere Pro switches angles where you press the number keys or click an angle. For music-driven edits, a plugin such as R Project Multicam AI plans the camera cuts from the beats, BPM and drops, and you review the result.

Should I flatten a multicam sequence?

Flatten only when the angle choices are final, for example before color grading or handing the project over. After flattening you can no longer switch angles on those clips without undoing it.
