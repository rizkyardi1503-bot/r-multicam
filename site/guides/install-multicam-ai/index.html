Install and activate R Project Multicam AI

By Rizki Ardiansyah, creator of R Project Multicam AI · Updated October 28, 2026

Use the same account on the website and in the Premiere Pro plugin. You can purchase a lifetime license before installing the extension.

New here? Follow the short first-edit checklist.

Download the public installer

A free R Project account is required to download the public Windows v2.3.11.16 or macOS v2.3.11.16 installer. Choose your operating system, create an account or sign in, confirm your email if you are new, then download the release ZIP.

Sign in to download Windows · v2.3.11.16Sign in to download macOS · v2.3.11.16

Install on Windows

Save your project and close Premiere Pro. Extract the release ZIP, run SETUP-WINDOWS.cmd, and follow the included instructions. Reopen Premiere after installation.

Install on macOS

Save your project and close Premiere Pro. Extract the release ZIP, run the included Install-macOS.command installer, and follow the release instructions. Reopen Premiere after installation.

Update an existing installation

Already installed? Click Check Update in the panel. On Windows, save your project and close Premiere when the installer asks. On macOS (v2.3.11.11 and newer) the update installs while Premiere is open and then asks you to restart Premiere. Every version before v2.3.11.11 must update; a Mac older than v2.3.11.11 needs one install from this website. After updating, open the panel once while online.

Create an account and purchase a license

Select Buy lifetime license on the website. New customers choose Create account, confirm their email, then return to sign in. Existing plugin customers use their current account. The displayed lifetime price is Rp2.000.000 one-time. The standard checkout amount is Rp2.000.000, or 10% less with an approved affiliate code. The final checkout amount is quoted and charged in IDR by Midtrans; review that rupiah amount before paying. No recurring plugin subscription. Adobe Premiere Pro is sold separately.

Use your license in Premiere

Open Window → Extensions (Legacy) → R Project Multicam AI. Click Continue with Google (v2.3.11.2+) or sign in with your existing email/password. Use the same account used for your purchase. Google login opens your system browser and returns to the plugin; keep Premiere open. The license badge shows TRIAL, LIFETIME or EXPIRED. Without a lifetime license you can also click Buy Lifetime in the panel: it shows the IDR amount and opens Midtrans checkout in your browser. The lifetime license is activated after server verification of payment; if it is still pending, click Check Payment in the panel or on the website. One device is active per account: the latest sign-in becomes active. A lifetime license keeps working offline for up to 72 hours after the panel was last opened online.

What the trial includes

The 7-day trial starts at the first plugin activation and includes panel steps 1 · Analyze Music, 3 · Auto Cut Multicam, 4 · Best Drops and 5 · Random Zoom. Step 2 · Sync Cameras to Music, which arranges your clips into a new synced sequence automatically, is for the lifetime license only. On the trial, or when your cameras are already in sync, skip step 2 and go from step 1 to step 3. See the quick start for the full workflow.

Check compatibility before purchasing

Installation and runtime behavior depend on your Premiere version, operating system and release package. Read the installer’s included requirements and instructions. Check Premiere and memory guidance. Mac v2.3.11.2 and newer enable Low Memory mode on machines with 12 GB or less, but do not guarantee large 4K project performance. Feature availability can vary by release.
