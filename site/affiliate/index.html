Affiliate program terms

Effective October 5, 2026. Applying means you accept these terms. Participation requires approval. R Project can suspend participation for fraud or misleading promotions.

  • The standard lifetime price is Rp3.575.000. A valid approved code gives a 10% discount, so the customer pays Rp3.217.500. One code per order, with no discount stacking.
  • Commission is 20% of the verified discounted IDR payment, rounded to whole rupiah. For example, a Rp3.217.500 verified payment earns Rp643.500. Provider fees do not change this commission calculation.
  • Attribution uses the code saved on the order. Referral links prefill the code; customers can change or remove it before checkout. No commission is earned for clicks, signups, unpaid orders or self-referrals.
  • Eligible commission becomes available 14 days after verified payment. Refunds and partial refunds void that order’s commission. Refunds after payout may require balance reconciliation before another withdrawal.
  • Minimum withdrawal is IDR 100,000. One request can be processing at a time. Bank details are saved with the request, and funds are reserved to prevent duplicate claims.
  • Payouts require owner review and a separate manual bank transfer. No instant or guaranteed payout time is promised. Contact support for bank destinations not supported by R Project.
  • Disclose your affiliate relationship when promoting the plugin. Do not promise features, results, compatibility or income that R Project has not confirmed.
  • These rates apply to this program version; already-created orders retain their recorded terms. Affiliates are responsible for any taxes applicable to their earnings.

Bank details are used to review and pay your withdrawal requests and are visible only to you and the program owner. Contact R Project support for corrections or withdrawal disputes.
