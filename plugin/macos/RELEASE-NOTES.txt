v2.3.11.14 PUBLIC — Sync, Auto Cut and Analyze Music fixes
New in this release:
- Sync Cameras to Music (Lifetime): drones sync through a camera filmed in one long take; a clip whose own audio
  clearly disagrees with its camera clock is placed by its audio; a long clip whose end does not match its start goes
  to Review; close matches are marked [CHECK] instead of high.
- Auto Cut Multicam: Cut Pace 2/4/6/8 no longer blocks Apply when the footage cannot keep every shot at 4 beats or
  more. The planner switches to another camera, moves the cut, or keeps the camera through a weaker drop next to a
  stronger one. Running it on a sequence that was already cut works with a warning.
- Auto Cut safety: Stop then Continue never removes footage where the winning camera was trimmed or deleted; a saved
  job resumes after reopening the project; Keep Clips is remembered after restarting Premiere; Apply stops before any
  edit when the backup copy cannot be made.
- Analyze Music: bars and downbeats are found per section (drops land on beat 1), tempo misreads in breakdowns and
  half-beat locks are fixed, the beat grid follows tempo changes between tracks, Best Drops ignores missing data, and a
  stalled FFmpeg read stops after 60 s with a clear message.
- Panel: every step folds to one line with its status; Sync Cameras to Music is Lifetime only (no longer beta).
- Account: a PC clock that is a few minutes or hours wrong no longer blocks access; the sign-in is stored encrypted
  (after going back to an older version, sign in once).
- Optional update for v2.3.11.11 and later. Macs older than v2.3.11.11 install once from r-multicam.pages.dev.

v2.3.11.9 PUBLIC — Music engine V19
New in this release:
- Music engine V19: a new beat tracker follows one continuous beat chain through the whole song, so cuts stay
  on the beat on long DJ sets and tempo changes.
- Visual quality: the AI director scores each camera for brightness, sharpness and shake (keyframes only,
  cached) and avoids dark, blurry or shaky moments.
- Faster: AI Vision checks large 4K camera files about 2x faster and several files at once on strong PCs;
  camera sync and beat analysis are faster too. A 4-camera 4K test went from 32 s to 17 s with identical
  beats, drops, sync offsets and cuts.
- Prices in the panel now match the website: Lifetime Rp2.000.000 one-time.
- The panel renews its sign-in shortly before it expires, so license checks no longer fail first and retry.
- Settings > FFmpeg: a saved FFmpeg that no longer runs is skipped and a working one is used.
- macOS installer: always installs the pinned, verified FFmpeg 9.0.2. An existing Homebrew FFmpeg is only used
  as a fallback, in place, and is no longer copied (a copy stopped working after "brew upgrade"). Apple Silicon
  is detected even when Terminal runs under Rosetta.
- Optional update: v2.3.11.2 and newer keep working. Delivered through Check Update. Save and close
  Premiere before installing.

v2.3.11.7 PUBLIC — Drone & FPV sync, exact Cut Pace and reliability
New in this release:
- Sync Cameras to Music (formerly Sync to Master Music; beta, lifetime license only): drone and FPV clips (no audio) are placed from their camera clock, bridged
  through a camera that synced by audio, on their own [CLOCK] track. Correct one clip with "Use selected
  clip" (or the nudge buttons) and every clip of that drone follows; the correction is remembered per drone
  and shoot date, so the next project of the same shoot needs no fix. Gyroflow FPV exports are remembered by
  their bin name. Build refuses when the open sequence or its A1 changed since Find camera sources.
- Simpler panel: five numbered steps in working order (Analyze Music, Sync Cameras to Music, Auto Cut
  Multicam, Best Drops, Random Zoom). Music detection runs with built-in settings, and the optional online
  AI music review was removed (analysis is always local).
- Cut Pace 2/4/6/8 now counts real beats: cuts land every selected number of beats, also when the beat
  detector misses or doubles beats, and stay on the beat when a second song starts on A1.
- No more "4-beat minimum" blocking Apply near drops; 2 Beat no longer holds one camera for a whole section.
- When a camera's footage ends inside a shot, the edit switches to the camera that continues, on a beat
  both cameras share.
- Music engine: tempo no longer locks at half speed (Best Drop windows have the promised length); drop
  spacing presets work; the optional online music review stays off until you enable it.
- Continue Multicam works after a stop that had already emptied a camera track; Stop before cutting no
  longer leaves a saved job behind; a sync left running by a closed panel is stopped; workflow presets set
  the right Cut Pace.
- Updates and installers: Windows auto-update works with non-English letters in the user name; the macOS
  installer waits for Premiere to quit; a pending update is shown after restarting Premiere; Windows
  backup and rollback work across drives; downloads are https only.
- Online lifetime checkout is closed for now, so Buy Lifetime is hidden. Trials and existing licenses are
  not affected.
- Optional update: v2.3.11.2 and newer keep working. Delivered through Check Update. Save and close
  Premiere before installing.

v2.3.11.6 PUBLIC — Sync to Master Music (beta), faster cleanup, Camera Role Contrast and reliability
New in this release:
- Sync to Master Music (beta, lifetime license): places every camera clip, including very short ones,
  at its position on the master music on A1, using each clip's own audio and the camera clock. It
  builds a NEW synced sequence; the current sequence is not changed. Clips it is unsure about go to a
  muted Review track; medium-confidence clips are marked [CHECK] for a quick look.
- Music engine: faster beat-drop detection; cuts land on the kick/bass entry; M4A/MP4 masters no longer
  cut about 2 frames late.
- Removing unused camera pieces is much faster on long sets (pieces are unlinked in batches).
- The backup copy of the sequence before Apply is now optional (checkbox under the main buttons; on by
  default). Turn it off on very long sets to save memory.
- Cleaner panel: Sync to Master Music and Podcast AI Director on the main screen, Continue Multicam
  shows when a job can be resumed, and the activity log moved to Help.
- Random Zoom keeps its v2.3.11.3 behaviour.
Also included (first public with this release):
- Camera Roles now steer the cut: the panel reads each role's shot size (wide / medium wide,
  medium, close / deck / detail) and direction (behind, left, right, front, top, crowd, side).
  Two angles of the same kind never follow each other (for example wide after wide). When no
  other covered camera exists, the shot continues instead of cutting to the same kind of angle.
- 4-beat cuts are kept at 25/30 fps (frame-rounded 4-beat shots are no longer merged).
- Music analysis of long sets is much faster with identical results; the music map is stored on
  disk only (no more browser-storage quota errors on long sets).
- Sequence In/Out is respected when applying an AI plan (before, Apply failed with
  "Edit point outside sequence In/Out").
- Stop works reliably; Windows: Continue after Stop works again (it reported "Camera media changed").
- Clips you disabled stay disabled; muted camera tracks are never touched; Keep Clips can be re-planned
  in the same Premiere session; manual-marker Begin works; many markers no longer disable the panel;
  unsupported frame rates are reported before any edit.
- No more stuck buttons after a slow Premiere response; longer limits for long sequences.
- macOS: no false "low memory" pauses; updates wait only for Premiere (not Photoshop/After Effects).
- Account: works offline for up to 72 hours after the last online check; two open panels no
  longer sign each other out; clear update and Google sign-in messages; promo codes at checkout.
- Installer: download timeouts, rollback of incomplete installs, clearer macOS privacy message.
- Supports Premiere Pro 2022 (v22) and newer.
- Optional update: v2.3.11.2 and newer keep working. Delivered through Check Update. Save and close
  Premiere before installing.

R Project Multicam AI v2.3.11.2 PUBLIC — macOS ONLY
Automatic Low Memory mode on macOS with 12 GiB RAM or less, including
M1 8 GB and Rosetta: CPU-only FFmpeg visual frame sampling, one decoding
thread, at most 24 optional visual samples, existing small timeline batches
and a 250 ms batch delay. Higher-memory Macs keep normal decoding policy.
macOS memory sampling estimates reclaimable VM pages instead of applying
Windows commit thresholds. Analyses preflight memory and check long audio
decoding periodically. Last-clip feature references are released early.
Beat/BPM/drop algorithms and timing resolution are unchanged. Google/manual
login, account identity, trial dates, paid licenses, camera coverage and
protected timeline cleanup remain intact. Native Mac performance unverified.
Save your project, quit Premiere completely, extract the ZIP, then run
Install-macOS.command. First test on a duplicate project. Keep the old
installer/installation backup for recovery. Proxies may still be needed.
Local diagnostic file: ~/Library/Application Support/RProjectMulticamAI/memory-diagnostics.json
Windows remains v2.3.11.2. Online minimum follows each OS public release,
with the existing zero-grace-period rule on the next entitlement check.
