02 / Random Zoom Active Scene READY
Run this after Multicam cleanup reaches 100%. Applies Scale animation to the remaining compatible active-scene clips on unlocked tracks.
Custom Scale: Every processed clip receives exactly two Scale keyframes. Zoom In = Minimum → Maximum. Zoom Out = Maximum → Minimum. Direction is randomized independently for every clip on every run.
Example: Min and Max are relative to each clip's own Scale: Min 100 / Max 115 on a clip at 100% gives 100% → 115%; on a clip at 50% it gives 50% → 57.5%. Re-running with Min 100 does not stack zooms. Still images, nested sequences, graphics, unavailable media, disabled clips and locked/muted tracks are skipped.
